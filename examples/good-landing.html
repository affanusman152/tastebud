The booking sheet, but it updates itself

Studios told us the paper sheet on the door was the only system everyone understood. So Kiln looks like that sheet: one row per wheel, one column per hour. Tap a free slot to book it. Running late? Release it, and the next person on the waitlist gets a text.

Kiln firings

Add your pieces to the next bisque or glaze firing and get a message when they're out.

Fair limits

Set a weekly cap per member so the regulars don't take every Saturday.

"We used to lose a wheel-hour a day to no-shows. Since the waitlist texts, it's almost none."
Dana Okafor, owner of Riverside Clay (42 members)
